Getting started

  1. Allow access to Apple Health so the app can read your lean mass, weight and workouts. If you use a Garmin scale or watch, turn on the Apple Health connection in Garmin Connect.
  2. Create an API key at platform.claude.com and paste it in the app under Settings › Claude. Analyses are billed to your own Anthropic account.
  3. Under Dishes, save your plates and bowls with their empty weight.
  4. Weigh the full plate, enter the weight, take a photo and tap Analyse.

Good to know

  • All values are estimates. They are not a diagnosis and do not replace medical or dietary advice.
  • If no fat-free mass or body weight arrives from Apple Health, enter them under Settings › Manual override.
  • Your data stays on your iPhone. See the privacy policy · Datenschutzerklärung.
