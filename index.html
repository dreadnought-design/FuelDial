Weigh. Snap. Done.

No portion guessing and no searching through food lists.

1

Weigh the plate

Save your plates and bowls once with their empty weight. Then put the full plate on the kitchen scale and enter what it shows.

2

Take a photo

Claude, Anthropic's AI model, recognises the foods and works out how the weighed amount splits across them.

3

Check and save

You see every item with grams, calories and nutrients. Correct anything you know better, then save.

One look tells you where you stand

The scale provides the weight, the photo the composition, your devices the rest.

Energy availability

In kcal per kg fat-free mass, with 30 and 45 as the benchmarks between too low and optimal.

Protein for your goal

Per kg body weight on the inner dial: 1.6–2.2 g for training, 1.8–2.7 g for weight loss.

What is still missing

The calories to reach an energy availability of 45 and the grams of protein to reach your range.

Macronutrients

Protein, carbohydrate and fat for the whole day and for each meal.

Seven days

Your daily energy availability for the past week, with both benchmarks drawn in.

Light or dark

Follows your iPhone, or pick the design you prefer.

Your data, from your devices

  • Fat-free mass, body weight and workouts come from Apple Health.
  • Using a Garmin scale or watch? Garmin Connect can share its data with Apple Health.
  • You can also enter fat-free mass and body weight by hand.

Private by design

  • No account, no advertising, no tracking.
  • Meals and photos are stored only on your iPhone.
  • A photo leaves your device only when you analyse it, and only after you have agreed. Privacy policy

What you need

Two things besides your iPhone.

A kitchen scale

The weight is measured, never guessed. That is what makes the estimate useful.

Your own Anthropic API key

The photo analysis runs through your Anthropic account and is billed there by Anthropic. The app itself is free.

All values are estimates based on photos, a kitchen scale and device data. FuelDial is not a medical device. It does not diagnose, treat or prevent any condition and does not replace medical or dietary advice.
